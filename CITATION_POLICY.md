# Citation Policy

Use the manuscript-specific citation printed in the final released paper when available.

Recommended series form:

> Asadi, A. (2026). *[Paper Title].* SubstrateX® Longitudinal Computational Dynamics Series, Version 1.0. SubstrateX® Longitudinal Labs.
